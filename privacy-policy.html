Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

Prakash Apartments ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit our website or use our services.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you voluntarily provide it through our website:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Apartment or Room Number (if applicable)
  • Feedback, complaints, maintenance requests, or other messages submitted through our forms
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We may also automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Type
  • Operating System
  • Date and Time of Visit
  • Pages Visited
  • General Website Usage Information

This information helps us improve our website and maintain its security.

2 How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • To process maintenance requests and tenant feedback.
  • To communicate important notices regarding the apartment.
  • To improve our services and website.
  • To maintain apartment management records.
  • To protect the safety and security of residents.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3 Information Sharing

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information may only be shared:

  • when required by applicable law;
  • with government or law enforcement authorities when legally required;
  • to protect the rights, property, or safety of Prakash Apartments, its residents, or others.

4 Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information collected through this website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5 Cookies

This website may use basic browser cookies or similar technologies necessary for normal website functionality.

We do not use cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking.

6 Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services to provide certain features or improve functionality. These services may have their own privacy policies. Examples may include:

  • GitHub Pages (Website Hosting)
  • Google Forms (Feedback Forms)
  • Google Maps
  • Google Fonts

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of these third-party services where applicable.

7 Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as necessary to:

  • provide apartment-related services;
  • maintain tenant records;
  • resolve disputes;
  • comply with legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be securely deleted.

8 User Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • access your personal information;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • update your information;
  • request deletion of your personal information where legally permitted.

Requests may be made using the contact details provided below.

9 Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10 External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites for your convenience.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Users should review the privacy policies of any external websites they visit.

11 Changes to this Privacy Policy

Prakash Apartments reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will become effective once they are published on this page.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
